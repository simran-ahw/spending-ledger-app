    Connect your phone

    Each bank or card notification on your Android phone is sent here the moment it arrives. Claude logs the purchase and you get a notification.

    Anyone with this link can add expenses to your ledger — keep it to yourself.

    Set up MacroDroid (about 5 minutes)
    1. In each bank or card app, turn on purchase alerts (minimum $0 if there's an option).
    2. Install MacroDroid from the Play Store and give it notification access when asked. Turn off battery optimisation for it.
    3. Add a macro. Trigger: Device Events → Notification → Notification Received → choose your bank and card apps.
    4. Action: Web Interactions → HTTP Request. Method POST, URL = your phone link above, content type text/plain. For the body, use the … button to insert the app name, the notification title and the notification text, separated by spaces.
    5. Save the macro. Your next purchase alert should appear in the app within seconds.
